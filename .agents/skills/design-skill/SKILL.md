# Enterprise Technical Design Rules

## Mandated Constraints
1. **Zero AI-Slop & Vibe Aesthetics**:
   - Never use purple/magenta decorative gradients.
   - Never use pill-shaped (`rounded-full`) buttons or inputs.
   - Never use fake metrics, fake reviews, or marketing hype.
   - Never use floating cursor animations, continuous pulsing effects, or over-the-top scroll animations.
   - Never use emojis anywhere in UI text, headings, badges, or console logs.
   - Never use em dashes (`—`) in copy; use hyphens, colons, or parentheses.

2. **Visual Density & Structural Layout**:
   - High information density designed for engineers and operators.
   - Use subtle 1px border dividers (`border-zinc-800`) to demarcate sections cleanly.
   - Ensure explicit visual alignment for tabular metrics and telemetry data.

3. **Required Legal & Operational Infrastructure**:
   - Valid SVG/PNG favicon on all pages.
   - Distinct, functional Privacy Policy (`/privacy`) and Terms of Service (`/terms`) pages.
   - No "Made with AI" labels or promotional tags.
